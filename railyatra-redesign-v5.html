Questions

The things people actually ask

When does Tatkal booking open?▾

Tatkal opens 24 hours before departure for AC classes and 10:00 AM the day before for non-AC, counted from the train's origin station. Premium Tatkal opens at the same times with dynamic pricing.

My money was debited but I have no ticket. What now?▾

Failed bookings with a debit are auto-reversed within 5–7 working days. You can track the exact stage under My Trips → Refunds.

What does RAC actually mean for my journey?▾

RAC guarantees a seat with a shared berth at night; it usually confirms into a full berth as cancellations come in before charting.

Can I change the boarding station after booking?▾

Yes, up to 24 hours before departure, once, through My Trips. The change is free but can't be reversed.
